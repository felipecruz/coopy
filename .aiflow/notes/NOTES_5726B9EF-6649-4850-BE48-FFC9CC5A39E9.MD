# F8: abort-exception behavior

Successful `@abort_exception` mutations are now journaled and published to replication subscribers. Failing decorated calls are omitted and their exceptions reach the caller. Combining `@readonly` with this decorator in either order still excludes both outcomes from journaling.

## Validate before mutating

The decorator does not roll back in-memory changes or external effects. Validate inputs before changing state; arrange compensation when needed. A later snapshot can preserve mutations from a failed call, while live replicas do not receive that failed action.

## Existing data and validation

Previously omitted successful calls cannot be recovered from absent journal entries. Preserve any still-live state with a snapshot before relying on restart recovery.
