Roadmap
=======

The roadmap lives in `ROADMAP.md`_ in the repository. It lists every feature
with its status, acceptance criteria, implementation commits and known
limitations, grouped by release. Longer-term directions, including
distribution and decentralization, are in `FUTURE.md`_; application ideas are
in `IDEAS.md`_.
